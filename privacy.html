Booklet Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Booklet is a private personal record management application. This policy explains how information is handled when the application is used.

2. Information collected

Depending on the features used, Booklet may store customer names, garment details, order dates, due dates, work status, measurements, pattern photographs and reminders entered by the application owner.

3. Google Sign-In

Booklet uses Google Sign-In to authenticate the application owner. Google account information is used for authentication and access control.

4. Google Drive

If photo storage through Google Drive is enabled, photographs selected by the owner may be stored in the owner's Google Drive. Access is subject to the owner's Google account permissions and the application's configured access.

5. Data storage

Application records may be stored using Google Firebase Firestore. Photographs may be stored separately in Google Drive. Data handling depends on the features configured and enabled in the application.

6. Data sharing

Booklet is intended for private use. Personal records are not intended to be sold or shared for advertising purposes. Data may be processed by Google services used to operate the application, subject to Google's applicable policies.

7. Data security

Reasonable steps will be taken to protect application records through authentication and access restrictions. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data deletion

The application owner can manage or delete records and photographs using the available application and Google account controls. Deletion from one service may not automatically delete copies stored elsewhere.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Booklet features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the Google account used to manage Booklet.
