Booklet

Your personal customer and work record book.

Welcome to Booklet

Booklet is a private record management application designed to help its owner organise customer details, garment records, measurements, pattern photographs, work progress and reminders in one place.

What Booklet offers

• Customer-wise records

• Individual garment and piece tracking

• Measurement and pattern photographs

• Work status and due-date tracking

• Personal reminders and calendar organisation

Private access

Booklet is intended for private use by its owner. Google sign-in is used to authenticate access to the application.

Privacy

Your records are intended to remain private. Please read our Privacy Policy for information about data handling.
